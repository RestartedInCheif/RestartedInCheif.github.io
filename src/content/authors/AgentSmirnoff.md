bio: 'It is said that rubbing alcohol treats outside wounds, but drinking alcohol treats the inside ones. At least, that seems to be the philosophy behind this sage. Hailing from the Great White North, Agent Smirnoff appears to be a man in his prime, and yet his tipsy state reflects the reality of this senior citizen. Confined to a walker due to a car accident, this agent spends his days polishing minerals and collecting disability. Now, you might be thinking that this old man’s misfortune led him down the path of alcoholism, but he was always like this. Before his crippled days, this handsome devil would fumble the most divine gifts from above when women approached him. The most frustrating demonstration being when a Lacrosse player held his hand in a bar, and this half wit moved his hand out of the way, thus slamming shut a door in his own face. Unlike his failed counterpart Icarus, Agent Smirnoff suffers not from a lack of ferocity, but from a lack of confidence. But this trait has also proven to be an asset as he plays the role of quartermaster skillfully, equipping this group with anything cheap, mostly from Temu and AliExpress. Without this commie, this group would be stranded in the middle of nowhere, confused and alone till death do us part.'
